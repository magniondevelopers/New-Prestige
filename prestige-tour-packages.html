The Process

How a Prestige Tour Package Works

From the first conversation to stepping off the flight back home — here is exactly how we make it happen.

You Tell Us Where & When
Call or WhatsApp our team with your destination, travel dates, number of travellers, and any preferences. That is your only job at this stage.
We Build the Itinerary
Our team creates a customised day-by-day itinerary based on your preferences, duration, and travel style — with accommodation, transport, and key experiences included.
You Approve & We Book
Review the itinerary, give us your go-ahead, and our team makes all the bookings — accommodation, transport, and any pre-arranged experiences at the destination.
You Travel. We Support.
You receive your full travel plan before departure. Our team remains reachable throughout your trip — for questions, changes, or any support you need while travelling.

What Typically Comes in a Package

  • Accommodation — vetted hotels and resorts at the destination, suited to your budget and preferences
  • Transport — intercity travel arrangements and local transport at the destination
  • Sightseeing — key attractions and experiences included in the itinerary
  • Day-by-day itinerary — so you always know what's planned and what's next
  • Team support — reachable before and during your trip for any questions or adjustments
  • Meals — inclusions vary by package and destination (shared during consultation)

Exact inclusions vary by destination and package type. Our team will share the complete details for your specific trip during the planning consultation.
