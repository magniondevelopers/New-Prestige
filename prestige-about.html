Our Story

Where It All Began

Prestige Vacations was born out of a simple but powerful belief — that every family in Chennai deserves a holiday that feels effortless, memorable, and genuinely worth it.


Over 15 years ago, we started with a single resort and a single promise — to take care of everything, so our members could just show up and enjoy. What began as a small holiday membership company in Chennai has grown into a full-service hospitality group trusted by over 14,000 families across India.


Today, Prestige Clubs & Resorts owns and operates three unique properties — The Country Resort and The Country Suites in Mahabalipuram, and the newly constructed The Country Bay at Marakkanam — with more in the pipeline. Our members get access not just to our own properties, but to a world of curated resort stays, events, day outings, and tour packages across India.


What has never changed in 15 years is the warmth. The team that picks up the phone. The staff at the resort who remembers your name. The feeling — every single time — that you made the right choice when you became a Prestige member.

Our Journey

15 Years of Milestones

Every year has brought new members, new properties, and new memories. Here is the Prestige journey — told in the moments that shaped us.

2009

The Beginning

Prestige Vacations was founded in Chennai with a bold vision — to make stress-free, affordable holidays accessible to every family in the city through a membership model.

2012

First Property

The Country Resort, Mahabalipuram was launched — giving Prestige its first owned resort property on the ECR, just 90 minutes from Chennai. Members now had a true resort destination to call their own.

2015

5,000 Members

A major milestone — Prestige crossed 5,000 active members, cementing its position as Chennai's most trusted holiday membership company. Tour packages expanded to cover 30+ destinations across India.

2018

The Country Suites

The Country Suites, Mahabalipuram was launched — private villas with personal pool access and indoor games, taking the Prestige experience to a new level of luxury and privacy.

2022

10,000 Members

Prestige crossed the 10,000-member milestone — a testament to the trust families across Chennai and Tamil Nadu had placed in the brand over more than a decade of consistent, genuine service.

2025

14,000+ & The Country Bay

With 14,000+ active members and The Country Bay at Marakkanam now newly constructed — featuring private beach access and a swimming pool — Prestige looks ahead to its most exciting chapter yet.
