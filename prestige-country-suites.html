About The Country Suites

When You Want More Than a Room

The Country Suites is a different kind of Prestige experience. Where The Country Resort gives you warmth and community, The Country Suites gives you something rarer — complete privacy.


Set in Mahabalipuram and operated by Prestige Clubs & Resorts, The Country Suites is a collection of private villas — each designed for families, couples, and groups who want their own space, their own pool, and their own pace. No shared corridors, no lobby crowds. Just you, your people, and a beautiful private space just 90 minutes from Chennai.


It is the kind of stay that Prestige Vacations members ask about once — and then keep coming back for. An intimate, exclusive experience that does not feel like a hotel. It feels like your own holiday home on the ECR.

The Centrepiece Experience

Your Own Private Pool

The defining feature of The Country Suites is simple — your own swimming pool. Not a shared resort pool with timings and crowds. Your own pool, available whenever you want it, accessible only to you and your group.

It changes the entire holiday experience. Morning swim before breakfast. An afternoon floating with no agenda. An evening in the water as the sun sets over Mahabalipuram. All yours, all private, all at your pace.

Completely Private
Your pool is exclusive to your villa — no shared access, no scheduled timings, no crowds. Use it whenever you want, however you want.
All Day Access

The Experience

What Makes The Country Suites Different

The Country Suites is not just an upgrade from a resort room. It is an entirely different kind of holiday.

Total Privacy

A private villa means no shared spaces, no lobby traffic, no neighbours you did not invite. The Country Suites is your exclusive retreat — from the moment you arrive to the moment you leave.

Your Own Pool

Every villa comes with personal swimming pool access. This alone changes the holiday completely — no timings, no sharing, no waiting. Just swim whenever you feel like it.

Indoor Games Room

The indoor games facility means rainy afternoons or slow evenings are never boring. Families, groups, and couples find themselves laughing together in ways that no hotel room can create.

Prestige Hospitality

All the warmth and service quality of Prestige Clubs & Resorts — the team that 14,000+ members trust — delivered in a private villa setting. The best of both worlds.

Play & Leisure

Entertainment Area

A comfortable entertainment space for movie time, music, or simply relaxing together — the perfect wind-down after a long day at the pool.
