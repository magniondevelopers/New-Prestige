The Prestige New Year Gala — An Evening to Remember

Every year, Prestige Vacations hosts its flagship New Year Eve celebration at The Country Resort, Mahabalipuram — and every year, members who have been once come back for the next. Live entertainment, gourmet dining, a festive atmosphere, and the warmth of the Prestige community gathered in one place as the year turns.

Members get first access — before any public ticket release. It is one of the most sought-after events on the Chennai resort calendar, and it fills up every year.
