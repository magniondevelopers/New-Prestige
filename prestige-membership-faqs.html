About Prestige Vacations

What is Prestige Vacations and how long have you been operating?

Prestige Vacations is a Chennai-based holiday membership company that has been operating for over 15 years. We are part of the Prestige Group of Companies, operating under two divisions — Prestige Vacations (membership, tours, and member experience) and Prestige Clubs & Resorts (our own resort properties).

We currently have 14,000+ active members across India and own three resort properties — The Country Resort and The Country Suites in Mahabalipuram, and The Country Bay in Marakkanam.

Is Prestige Vacations a trustworthy company? What do existing members say?

We have been serving Chennai families for over 15 years with 14,000+ active members — that number alone reflects the trust our members place in us. We own all our resort properties (The Country Resort, The Country Suites, and The Country Bay), which means consistent quality and direct accountability.

You can read genuine member experiences on our testimonials page, on Google Reviews, MouthShut, TripAdvisor, and JustDial. Our members regularly share their experiences across these platforms.

What is the difference between Prestige Vacations and Prestige Clubs & Resorts?

Both are part of the same Prestige Group of Companies — they are two divisions of the same organisation.

  • Prestige Vacations — the membership and holiday experience division. Manages membership plans, tour packages, events, and member support.
  • Prestige Clubs & Resorts — the hospitality and property division. Owns and operates The Country Resort, The Country Suites, and The Country Bay.

When you become a member, you get access to both — the experiences managed by Prestige Vacations and the properties owned by Prestige Clubs & Resorts.

Where is the Prestige Vacations office located?

Our main office is in Chennai. We also have presence in Velachery for member walk-ins and enquiries. To get the current office address and directions, please call us on 1800 121 3457 (toll free) or WhatsApp us and we'll share the details immediately.

Membership Plans

What membership plans does Prestige Vacations offer?

Prestige Vacations offers two membership plans:

  • Club Membership — access to all Prestige properties, day outings, priority event invitations, member discounts, and gift voucher access. Ideal for families who want flexible, ongoing access.
  • Holiday Membership — everything in the Club Membership, plus annual resort stay nights and curated tour packages to 50+ destinations across India. The most complete holiday plan available.
Compare Both Plans
Is the Prestige Vacations membership worth it?

For families who holiday regularly and value convenience, the membership pays for itself quickly. Here is what makes it genuinely worth it:

  • You get access to three owned resort properties — resort stays at member rates are significantly lower than non-member rates.
  • Tour packages to 50+ destinations are included in the Holiday Membership — each package individually would cost considerably more.
  • Day outings, events, and discounts across the year add up in value far beyond the membership cost.
  • You stop planning from scratch every holiday — the Prestige team handles it for you.

The honest answer: if you holiday 2–3 times a year, the membership will save you money while giving you better experiences.

How is Prestige Vacations different from just booking a resort directly?

Booking a resort directly gives you a single stay. The Prestige membership gives you a holiday lifestyle:

  • Access to three owned properties at member rates — significantly cheaper than public booking rates.
  • Tour packages to 50+ destinations — no separate planning or booking needed.
  • Year-round day outings with pool, dining, and resort access.
  • Exclusive events throughout the year — not available to the public.
  • A dedicated team that knows you by name and handles all planning.
  • Gift vouchers for family and corporate gifting.

The membership eliminates the friction of planning every holiday from scratch. You simply decide where you want to go and call us. We do the rest.

How far are the Prestige properties from Chennai?
  • The Country Resort, Mahabalipuram — approximately 60 km from Chennai city centre, about 90 minutes by road via the ECR.
  • The Country Suites, Mahabalipuram — same area as The Country Resort, approximately 90 minutes from Chennai.

Bookings & Stays

How do I book a stay or day outing as a Prestige member?

Booking as a Prestige member is straightforward. Simply contact our team:

  • Call our toll-free number: 1800 121 3457
  • WhatsApp us: +91 80012 13457
  • Email us: info@prestigevacations.in

Tell us the property you want to visit, the dates, and the number of guests. Our team will check availability and confirm your booking. For day outings, prior booking is recommended to ensure availability, especially on weekends and public holidays.

How far in advance should I book a resort stay?

We recommend booking at least:

  • Weekdays — 3–5 days in advance
  • Weekends — 7–10 days in advance
  • Public holidays and long weekends — 2–4 weeks in advance
  • New Year, Christmas, Diwali — 1–2 months in advance

As a member, you get priority booking rights — but popular dates fill up fast, especially at The Country Suites and The Country Bay. We always recommend booking early.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Our cancellation policy varies based on the type of booking and the notice given. Generally:

  • Cancellations with adequate notice are rescheduled without penalty.
  • Last-minute cancellations may be subject to terms based on the specific booking.
  • For event bookings and peak season stays, specific cancellation terms apply.

For precise, current cancellation policy details, please contact our team directly — we are always happy to work with members on reschedules.

Gift Vouchers

What is a Prestige Vacations gift voucher and how does it work?

A Prestige Vacations gift voucher is a prepaid voucher that can be redeemed for experiences across all our properties — resort stays, day outings, events, tour packages, and more. The recipient contacts our team, chooses the experience they want, and we arrange it.
