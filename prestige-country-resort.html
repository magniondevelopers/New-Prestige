About The Property

A Resort That Feels Like Home — But Better

The Country Resort in Mahabalipuram is the flagship property of Prestige Clubs & Resorts — a unit of Prestige Vacations. Nestled along the East Coast Road, surrounded by natural beauty and just minutes from the UNESCO World Heritage Mahabalipuram Shore Temple, the resort is designed to offer a perfect blend of rustic charm and modern comfort.


From the moment you arrive, the warmth is unmistakable. Our spacious rooms and suites are thoughtfully designed with plush beds, cosy linens, and panoramic views of the surrounding landscape. Our on-site restaurant, Green Bay, serves fresh gourmet cuisine made with locally-sourced ingredients. Our team — warm, attentive, and genuinely invested in your experience — makes every visit feel personal.


Whether you are a Prestige Vacations member coming for your annual stay, a family seeking a weekend escape from Chennai, or a couple looking for a quiet retreat — The Country Resort is where great memories are made.

Dining at The Country Resort

Green Bay Restaurant

The Green Bay restaurant at The Country Resort is more than just a dining experience — it is a destination in itself. Named after its picturesque setting, Green Bay offers a delightful culinary journey with views and a menu that reflects the very best of the region.

Fresh Seafood — Locally sourced, freshly prepared seafood dishes that celebrate the flavours of the Mahabalipuram coast.
Vegetarian Options — A rich selection of vegetarian dishes prepared with fresh, locally sourced ingredients for every palate.
Live Aquarium — Dine with an extensive live aquarium display — a unique dining feature that makes every meal memorable.
Sea Breeze Setting — Enjoy your meal in the soothing ambience of the sea breeze with the resort's natural surroundings as your backdrop.
Complimentary Breakfast — All resort stays include a complimentary breakfast — the perfect start to every morning at The Country Resort.
