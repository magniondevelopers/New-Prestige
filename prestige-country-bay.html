About The Country Bay

Where the Sea Becomes Your Backyard

The Country Bay is Prestige Clubs & Resorts' most ambitious and exciting property yet. Newly constructed on the Marakkanam coastline — one of Tamil Nadu's most beautiful stretches of beach — The Country Bay brings together private beach access, a stunning swimming pool, and the genuine warmth of 15 years of Prestige hospitality.


This is not just another resort near Chennai. The Country Bay is a beachfront retreat where the sound of the waves is constant, the horizon is unobstructed, and your mornings start with the sea. Something that no property in the Prestige family has offered before.


For Prestige Vacations members, The Country Bay adds a completely new dimension to the membership — a private coastal escape that joins The Country Resort and The Country Suites to give members the most complete holiday portfolio of any Chennai-based membership company.
