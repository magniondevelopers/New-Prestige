What It Covers

Where Can a Prestige Gift Voucher Be Used?

Prestige gift vouchers are redeemable across all our properties and experiences — maximum flexibility for the recipient.

Resort Stays
Redeem for an overnight stay at The Country Resort, The Country Suites, or The Country Bay. The recipient picks the property and the date.
Day Outings
A perfect day at any Prestige resort — pool access, dining, and leisure. Great for a single-day gift that still feels complete.
Tour Packages
Holiday Membership holders can apply gift vouchers toward tour packages to 50+ destinations across India.
Events
Redeem for event registrations — New Year gala tickets, seasonal celebrations, and special member events throughout the year.
Weddings & Celebrations
Apply the voucher toward a wedding venue booking, anniversary dinner, or private celebration at any Prestige property.
Membership Contribution
Gift vouchers can also be applied as a contribution toward a Prestige Vacations membership — perfect for gifting the membership experience itself.
