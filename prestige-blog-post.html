Is the Prestige Vacations Membership Worth It? An Honest 2025 Review

The question we get asked more than any other — on the phone, on WhatsApp, in person at our Velachery office — is this: "Is the Prestige Vacations membership actually worth it?"

It is a fair question. A holiday membership is a financial commitment. You are paying upfront for something you have not yet experienced. And the internet is full of contradictory opinions — some enthusiastic, some sceptical, some that are clearly about a different company entirely.

So here, in one article, is our most complete and honest answer. No sales pitch. Just what the membership is, what it costs, what you actually get, and what 14,000+ members have told us over 15 years.

What Is the Prestige Vacations Membership?

Prestige Vacations is a Chennai-based holiday membership company, part of the Prestige Group of Companies. The company operates in two divisions:

  • Prestige Vacations — manages membership plans, tour packages, events, and member experience.
  • Prestige Clubs & Resorts — owns and operates three resort properties: The Country Resort and The Country Suites in Mahabalipuram, and The Country Bay in Marakkanam.

When you become a member, you get access to both. A membership is not a subscription to a booking portal. It is a long-term relationship with a company that owns the properties you visit and manages the experiences you enjoy.

The Country Resort, Mahabalipuram — one of three owned Prestige properties included in the membership.

The Two Membership Plans Explained

Prestige Vacations offers two membership plans. Understanding the difference is the most important part of choosing correctly.

Club Membership

The Club Membership gives you access to all Prestige properties, day outings, priority event invitations, member discounts, and gift voucher access. It is the right plan if you want flexible, ongoing access to the resort properties without committing to specific tour packages.

Holiday Membership

The Holiday Membership includes everything in the Club Membership, plus annual resort stay nights and curated tour packages to 50+ destinations across India. If your family takes 2–3 holidays per year and you want those holidays properly planned, this is the plan to consider.

What You Actually Get as a Member

Beyond the plan specifics, what does the Prestige membership actually look like day-to-day? Here is what 14,000+ members consistently tell us they use the most:

  1. Day outings on the ECR — the most-used benefit. Drive down on a Saturday morning, spend the day at the pool, have lunch at Green Bay, drive back refreshed. Members do this monthly.
  2. Weekend resort stays — Friday to Sunday at Mahabalipuram or Marakkanam at rates significantly below public booking prices.
  3. Annual events — the New Year Gala, Pongal, Diwali, and Family Fun Day are consistently mentioned as highlights members look forward to every year.
  4. Tour packages — Holiday Members use one or two tour packages per year. The value becomes obvious when you compare the cost of self-organising the same trip.
"We joined four years ago. In those four years, we have been to Ooty, Kerala, Andaman, and Goa through the Prestige tour packages. The membership cost was covered in the first trip alone." — Rajesh & Priya Nair, Holiday Members, Pallikaranai

How Much Does It Cost?

Membership pricing is not published on our website — and here is the honest reason why. The right plan and the right price depend on your family's size, how often you holiday, and what you want from the membership. Quoting a single number would be misleading.

The honest answer on pricing

EMI and instalment options are available. The specific pricing, plan tenure, and payment options are discussed transparently during your membership consultation — no pressure, no hidden fees, no surprises. The best way to get accurate current pricing is to call or WhatsApp our team directly.

What we can say honestly: for families who take 2–3 holidays per year, the Holiday Membership typically pays for itself within 12–18 months. The Club Membership is often worth it from the first year of regular day outings alone.

What Real Members Say

We have 14,000+ active members. Here is what the ones we hear from most consistently say — positive and, where relevant, constructively critical:

What members say they love

  • The day outings — "most used and most valued benefit" (multiple members)
  • The New Year Gala — "we book it the moment it opens every year" (consistent feedback)
  • The tour planning — "they handled everything, we just showed up" (Holiday Members)
  • The team — "they know us by name after a few visits" (multiple properties)

What members say could be better

  • Weekend availability books up fast — booking early is essential
  • Some members wish for more destinations in the tour package catalogue

Our Honest Verdict

The Prestige Vacations membership is worth it if you are the kind of family that holidays regularly — or wants to. It removes the friction of planning, gives you access to three owned properties at member rates, and connects you to a community of families with similar values around travel and quality time together.

If you holiday once a year and prefer to book independently, the membership may not be necessary. But if you value convenience, consistency, and the feeling of always having somewhere beautiful to go — Prestige delivers on that promise with 15 years of evidence.

The best way to decide is to speak with our team. There is no obligation, no pressure, and if it is not the right fit for your family, we will tell you honestly.
