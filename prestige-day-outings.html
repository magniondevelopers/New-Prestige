The Concept

A Full Vacation. In a Single Day.

A Prestige day outing is not a casual visit. It is a carefully designed day experience that gives you everything a resort stay offers — pool access, resort-quality dining, beautiful grounds, leisure activities, and genuine service — all without the need to book an overnight stay.


Drive down from Chennai in the morning. Spend the day in and around the pool. Have a proper lunch at the resort restaurant. Walk the grounds, unwind, breathe. Drive back in the evening feeling like you took a full vacation. Not a day off. A real reset.


Available year-round at all three Prestige properties — The Country Resort, The Country Suites, and The Country Bay. Members get priority access and exclusive day outing rates.

A Typical Day

How a Perfect Day Outing Looks

Every day outing is yours to shape — but here is what a great Prestige day looks like from start to finish.

Morning · 8:00 – 9:30 AM

Leave Chennai, Arrive Refreshed

Drive down the ECR — one of Chennai's most scenic coastal roads. 90 minutes of easy driving through the coast, and you arrive at the resort feeling like the city is already far away.

Late Morning · 9:30 – 11:00 AM

Check In & Settle

Our team welcomes you in. Get settled, freshen up, and start with a tea or coffee at the restaurant. Take your time — the day is entirely yours.

Midday · 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM

Pool Time

The pool is the heart of a Prestige day outing. Swim, float, splash with the kids, or just sit poolside with a cold drink. The pool area is well-maintained, spacious, and genuinely enjoyable for all ages.

Afternoon · 1:30 – 3:00 PM

Lunch at the Restaurant

A proper lunch — not resort fast food. At The Country Resort, the Green Bay restaurant serves fresh seafood, vegetarian options, and a full à la carte menu. The kind of meal that makes an afternoon feel slow and good.

Post-Lunch · 3:00 – 5:30 PM

Unwind & Explore

Walk the resort grounds. Play an indoor game. Go back to the pool for a late afternoon dip. This is the part of the day that feels most like a holiday — slow, aimless in the best possible way, completely unhurried.

Evening · 5:30 – 6:00 PM

Drive Back Refreshed

Pack up, say goodbye to the team, and get back on the ECR. You'll be back in Chennai by dinner — feeling like you actually rested. That is the entire point of a Prestige day outing.

Insider Tips

Make the Most of Your Day Outing

Arrive Before 10 AM

Getting there early means you have the pool and grounds almost to yourself for the first couple of hours. The midday period is the busiest — arriving early gives you the best of the day in peace.

Book in Advance for Weekends

Day outing slots on weekends and public holidays fill up. Call or WhatsApp us 3–5 days ahead to secure your slot — especially for groups of 8 or more.

Pack Light — The Resort Has Everything

You do not need much — swimwear, a change of clothes, and sunscreen. The resort has changing facilities, towels on request, and everything else you will need for a comfortable day.

Weekdays Are Quieter — and Better

If you can manage a weekday visit, you will have the pool, the restaurant, and the grounds with far fewer guests. The experience is calmer, the service is more attentive, and the whole day feels more like a private retreat.
