Our Celebration Services

Everything Arranged. Nothing Overlooked.

Tell us your vision. Our team translates it into a celebration that runs perfectly — so you are never managing logistics on the day itself.

Venue Setup & Décor

Our team sets up the venue — tables, seating, lighting, and basic décor — according to your requirements. For custom floral and theme décor, we coordinate with trusted vendors who know our properties well.

In-House Catering

Our kitchen prepares all food in-house. South Indian, North Indian, Continental — or a curated multi-cuisine spread. Fresh, high-quality food served by our own team. Menu options are shared during your planning consultation.

Guest Accommodation

Rooms and villas at the property for your guests to stay overnight. We block rooms for your celebration date so family and friends do not need to worry about travel after a late evening.

Entertainment Coordination

From live music to DJs to cultural performances — we help coordinate entertainment for your event. We work with trusted partners who have performed at Prestige properties before and know the space well.

Photography Coordination

We can recommend photographers who know our properties — so your celebration photographs are made for the setting, using the natural light, the gardens, and the resort backdrops to their full effect.

Dedicated Event Coordinator

One dedicated coordinator for your celebration — from the first planning call to the day itself. One contact. One person who knows every detail of your event and is present on the day to make sure everything runs perfectly.

How It Works

From First Call to Perfect Celebration

Our planning process is designed to take the stress completely out of celebrating. Here is exactly what to expect.

01

Initial Enquiry

Call or WhatsApp our team with your occasion type, approximate date, expected number of guests, and preferred venue. We respond within 24 hours.

02

Venue & Date

We confirm availability for your date and venue, walk you through the options, and schedule a property visit if you'd like to see the space before booking.

03

Plan Together

A detailed planning conversation — menu, setup, guest accommodation, entertainment, and special requirements. We put together a complete proposal for your approval.

04

Confirm & Relax

Confirm the booking, and hand it over to us. Our team manages all the coordination from here. You focus on being excited about your celebration.

05

Celebrate

Arrive at the resort. Your coordinator is there. The setup is done. The food is ready. All you need to do is be present and celebrate.
