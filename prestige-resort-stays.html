Insider Tips

Getting the Most from Your Stay

Book Early for Weekends

Weekend availability — especially at The Country Suites and The Country Bay — fills up fast. Book at least 7–10 days ahead for weekends and 4–6 weeks for long weekends and public holidays.

Weekday Stays Are Underrated

Quieter, more peaceful, and more available. If you can take a day off, a Tuesday–Thursday stay is a completely different — and often better — experience than a crowded Friday night.

Stay During an Event Weekend

Plan your stay around a Prestige event — especially the New Year Gala — and your resort stay becomes a full experience. Some of our most memorable stays happen on event weekends.
