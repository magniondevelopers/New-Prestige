What Holiday Membership Includes

Six pillars that make the Holiday Membership the most complete holiday plan available in Chennai.

Annual Resort Stay Nights

Every year, your membership includes a set number of resort stay nights at Prestige properties — use them however you like, across any of our three locations.

  • Stay at The Country Resort, Mahabalipuram
  • Stay at The Country Suites (private villas)
  • Stay at The Country Bay, Marakkanam
  • Nights rollover with prior arrangement

Tour Packages — 50+ Destinations

The defining feature of the Holiday Membership — curated tour packages to 50+ destinations across India, planned and managed by our team.

  • Hill stations — Ooty, Kodaikanal, Munnar
  • Beaches — Goa, Kerala, Andaman
  • Heritage — Rajasthan, Agra, Mysuru
  • Spiritual — Varanasi, Tirupati, Shirdi

Day Outings — Year Round

Pool access, dining, and resort facilities at all Prestige properties — available year-round whenever you want a quick escape from Chennai.

  • No advance booking required for most dates
  • All three properties accessible
  • Member-only day access pricing
  • Pool, dining, grounds & facilities

Exclusive Events

Priority access to all Prestige member events throughout the year — New Year galas, seasonal festivals, family fun days, and special occasions.

  • New Year Eve gala — first access
  • Seasonal & festival celebrations
  • Family fun days and weekend events
  • Member-only event pricing

Gift Vouchers

Purchase and redeem Prestige gift vouchers — for birthdays, anniversaries, festivals, and corporate gifting. Holiday Members get exclusive voucher pricing.

Dedicated Member Support

A Prestige team that handles your bookings, plans your tours, and ensures every single experience is exactly what you expected — and usually more.
