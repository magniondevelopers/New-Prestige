What We Arrange

Everything Your Team Needs, Handled

We manage the entire corporate outing — from logistics to meals to activities. Your only job is to show up and enjoy the day with your team.

Pool & Resort Access

Full pool access for the team, resort grounds, and leisure facilities. The pool is the social centrepiece of every day outing — it works across every team size and every industry.

Group Catering

In-house group catering — South Indian meals, North Indian, Continental, or multi-cuisine buffets. Customised to your team's dietary preferences. No outside caterers, no coordination overhead.

Team Activities

Indoor games, outdoor activities, and informal team experiences. We help structure the day so there is the right mix of activity and downtime — neither over-programmed nor underplanned.

Meeting & Workshop Space

For offsites that include working sessions, we provide dedicated meeting space — away from the distraction of the office, in an environment that actually encourages clear thinking.

Accommodation

For overnight retreats, we block rooms and villas for your team at group rates. Staying on-site removes end-of-day logistics and lets the team time extend naturally into the evening.

Parking & Transport

Ample parking for large groups. For teams using chartered transport or buses, our property can accommodate group arrivals with ease. We confirm all logistics during the planning call.

How It Works

Planning Your Corporate Outing

From first call to the team bus pulling into the resort — our planning process is designed to be simple, fast, and completely off your plate.

01

Tell Us the Brief

Team size, preferred date, type of outing (day visit, retreat, annual day), and any specific requirements. One call or WhatsApp message is enough to start.

02

We Send a Proposal

Our team puts together a detailed proposal — venue, programme, catering, accommodation if needed, and pricing. Sent to you within 48 hours of the initial enquiry.

03

Finalise & Confirm

Review the proposal, share any changes, and confirm the booking. Once confirmed, we lock in the date and begin coordinating everything on our end.

04

Your Team Shows Up

On the day, our coordinator is on-site. Everything is set up. Your team arrives to a ready resort — and a day that feels effortlessly well-organised.
