The Club Membership — Flexible. Rewarding. Always Worth It.

The Prestige Club Membership is designed for families and individuals who want ongoing access to the Prestige lifestyle — without being tied to a fixed number of annual nights.


As a Club Member, you get access to all three Prestige properties for day outings, events, and resort experiences, along with priority invitations to our curated events calendar — from New Year galas to seasonal celebrations.


It is the membership for families who want to use Prestige across the year in different ways — a day out this month, an event next month, a short stay next quarter. Complete flexibility, consistent quality.

Every Club Member gets access to the full Prestige experience — across all properties, all year round.

Pool access, resort facilities, and dining at all Prestige properties — a perfect day from Chennai without an overnight stay. Year-round availability.
